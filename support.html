Product and download support

Storefront status

This website links to the existing Ko-fi product listing but does not process payments or host paid files. The live price, checkout methods, and delivery configuration must be confirmed on Ko-fi.

Questions before purchase

Before purchasing, review the frequently asked questions, try the free 3-page sample PDF, and read the current product listing. No separate support email is published. For order or download issues, use the support/contact route available through Ko-fi or the purchase receipt, if provided.

After checkout launches

If you have already purchased, use the contact or support route shown on your Ko-fi receipt or account to report a missing link or corrupted file. Store-specific options depend on the platform. Do not include card details, passwords, or identity documents in support messages.

Protect your information

Do not send payment-card details, passwords, identity documents, or private information through public comments or social-media messages. This website does not process payments; checkout is handled by the external Ko-fi listing.
