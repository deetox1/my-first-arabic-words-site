What languages are included?

The workbook focuses on Arabic letters and first words with French meanings. Flashcards include English reference words alongside Arabic and French.

Does it teach every form of Arabic letters?

The workbook begins with isolated Arabic letters. Letter shapes can change depending on their position in a word, so this is an introductory familiarity resource rather than a complete Arabic curriculum.
